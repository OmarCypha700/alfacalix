What we heard

The brief

A software products and technology solutions company serving educational institutions, businesses and organisations, from Ghana outward. The identity must feel professional, intelligent, reliable and forward-thinking: a serious software company, never a gaming, crypto, telecoms or IT-repair look.

professionalintelligentreliableforward-thinkingsimple

Success: distinct from generic tech marks, recognisable without the name, clear at 16 px, works in one colour and reversed, credible to institutional decision-makers.

Final design

Held

The A of Alfa is carved out of a disc, and its crossbar breaks out through the edge to open the disc into the C of Calix: Alfa, held within Calix.

  • One idea, both initials: an A you read at once, a C you notice second
  • The crossbar breaking out reads as connection and forward movement without an arrow
  • The cyan core is what the company builds: the product at the heart of the institution
  • A solid disc survives 16 px favicons, app icons, avatars and one-colour print
  • Plus Jakarta Sans wordmark matches the company website
